Admissions

Begin Your Child’s Golden Journey

A warm welcome to prospective parents. This page explains how admission works, what to prepare, and how to arrange a visit to see our school in person.

Step by Step

The Admission Process

Six simple steps from your first enquiry to your child’s first day. Actual requirements and procedures are confirmed by school management for each applicant.

1

Submit an Enquiry

Complete the online enquiry form below, or call the school office on 0904 013 9800.

2

Speak With the School

We will discuss your child’s age, current class, and the most suitable placement.

3

Complete the Application Form

Obtain and complete the school’s official application form as directed by the office.

4

Submit Required Documents

Provide the documents requested by the school (see the guide below).

5

Assessment or Placement

Follow the school’s guidance on any assessment, interview, or placement process, where applicable.

6

Receive Confirmation

The school will confirm the outcome and next steps directly with you.

Be Prepared

Required Documents

The following guide helps families prepare. The final list for your child will be confirmed by the school office.

  • Completed official application form (provided by the school)
  • Copy of the child’s birth certificate
  • Most recent report from the child’s previous school, where applicable
  • Passport-sized photographs of the child (number to be confirmed)
  • Transfer or leaving certificate for mid-stream transfers, where applicable
  • Any additional documents requested by the school office
Please note: Do not send original documents until the school asks for them. Requirements above are a guide and must be confirmed by school management.

Helpful Answers

Admission Questions

Everything else parents usually ask us before applying.

Enquiries are welcome throughout the year. Available places depend on class capacity, so please call 0904 013 9800 for the current position.

Placement arrangements — which may include an assessment or interview for some classes — are explained by the school office when you enquire. No dates are published here to avoid out-of-date information.

For accurate and current fee information, please speak directly with the school office on 0904 013 9800. This ensures you always receive the correct figures for your child’s class.

Mid-term and mid-session transfers are considered subject to available places and placement. Please contact the school to discuss your circumstances.
