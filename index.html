Helpful Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for parents. For anything not covered here, please call the school and we will be glad to help.

Start by submitting an admission enquiry or calling the school on 0904 013 9800. The school office will guide you on the appropriate class, the official application form, and the documents required.

The school is located on Polo Road, near Polo Health Clinic. Please call ahead if you would like directions or wish to arrange a visit.

The school welcomes enquiries across its Early Years, Nursery, Primary, and Secondary stages. Because available places can change, please contact the school to confirm the right class for your child.

Requirements are confirmed by the school office for each applicant. Families are commonly asked for the completed application form and supporting documents such as a copy of the child’s birth certificate and previous school report where applicable. Please see the Admissions page and confirm the final list with the school.

Call us on 0904 013 9800, visit us on Polo Road, near Polo Health Clinic, or send an enquiry through the online enquiry form. Our official email address will be published on this website once confirmed by the school.

Yes — visits are arranged through the school office so we can welcome you properly. Please call 0904 013 9800 to agree a convenient time.

Important updates are shared directly with parents and guardians by the school, and general announcements will also appear on our News & Events page. Always confirm term dates and event details with the school office.

For accurate, up-to-date fee information, please contact the school office directly on 0904 013 9800. Fees are not published on this page so that families always receive the correct current details.
